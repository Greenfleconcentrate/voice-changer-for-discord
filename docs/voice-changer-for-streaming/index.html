Voice changer for streaming

This voice changer for live streaming works as your stream microphone. Your audience hears your new voice live, with no editing afterwards.

Download from SourceForge

Add it to OBS Studio

  1. Download Voice Changer and choose a voice model.
  2. In OBS Studio, go to Settings and Audio.
  3. Set Mic/Auxiliary Audio to Voice Changer, then check the level in the Audio Mixer.

Add it to Streamlabs

Open Settings, then Audio, and set Mic/Auxiliary Device to Voice Changer. Speak for a few seconds and watch the mixer to check that the signal is coming through.

A software voice changer microphone for streaming

You don't need extra hardware. Keep your current microphone: Voice Changer takes your voice, transforms it and passes it to your streaming app as a microphone.

Stream and play on the same PC

Games, streaming and AI voice conversion all need computing power. To keep your stream and game smooth, offload voice processing to another machine.
