Voice changer for Zoom

Use this voice changer for Zoom meetings and other video calls. Zoom treats it like any other microphone, so your changed voice goes straight into the call.

Download from SourceForge

Set it up before a Zoom meeting

  1. Download Voice Changer and choose a voice model.
  2. Open Zoom, then go to Settings and Audio.
  3. Under Microphone, select Voice Changer.
  4. Use Test Mic to hear how you sound before you join.

Switch during a meeting

Click the arrow next to Mute in the meeting controls and select Voice Changer from the microphone list. Choose your usual microphone from the same list to go back to your own voice.

Other video calls

Any video call app with a microphone setting works in the same way. Look for the audio or device settings and select Voice Changer as the input.

Be open with the people on the call

Use a changed voice only where it is welcome, and tell people when it matters. Never use it to pretend to be someone else.
