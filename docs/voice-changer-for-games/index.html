Voice changer for games

Use this free voice changer for games on PC. Once it is your microphone, your teammates hear your new voice in game voice chat.

Download from SourceForge

Set it up for gaming

  1. Download Voice Changer and choose a voice model.
  2. Open the game's audio settings and find the voice chat input option.
  3. Select Voice Changer as your microphone, then test it in a party or lobby.

Where to find the setting

Voice changer for RobloxMake it your default microphone in your system's sound settings

Keep your games running smoothly

AI voice conversion takes computing power. If you need that power for the game, offload the processing to another machine. Your PC keeps running the game while the second machine changes your voice.

Follow each game's rules

Check a game's rules and community guidelines before you use a changed voice, and never use it to harass or impersonate other players.
